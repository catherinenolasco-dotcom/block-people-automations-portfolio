Case studies

Five problems, and what changed.

People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge

There was no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and employee questions had no tracked channel beyond email and word of mouth.

What I did

Built a 20-25 SOP library with a named owner and backup for each procedure, launched ASKPeople (Jira and Confluence with tiered SLAs), and made Sapling the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and connected through Zapier.

Result

The function scaled from 3 countries to 5. Six consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits. Approval cycles dropped from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours. ASKPeople resolved 5-10 tickets a day within 24-48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process that created audit risk. Early feedback said a form would be too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form that requires documentation and approval before any HRIS update, plus Notion New Hire and Offboarding workflows with an owner on every task and automated Slack and email reminders.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed, and preboarding that starts within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

A reorganization affecting 18 employees across the US and India needed severance, COBRA, and total cost worked out, and leadership had never seen a cost model for one.

What I did

Built an AI-enabled severance and cost modeling tool with Claude and Gemini that calculated severance, COBRA cost, and COBRA months owed per employee, drafted the agreements, and tracked status on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

Leadership saw the full cost of a reorganization for the first time. Drafting time fell from 20-30 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total for all 18.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

30-60-90

My first 90 days.

A proposed plan built around the success picture in the role description.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet People leaders across operations, performance, compensation, talent, and executive communications to hear where the time goes.
  • Map the current workflows and the systems and data behind them before changing anything.
  • Rank pain points by hours saved and risk reduced, and agree the short list.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Simplify the top process on paper first, with named owners, checkpoints, and where human review stays.
  • Prototype the first tool with AI and put a working version in front of the people who will use it.
  • Agree how success is measured, with a baseline for speed and quality.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Release the first tool to a small group and collect feedback in the first weeks.
  • Track adoption and turnaround against the baseline, in the same way the Employee Action Form was tracked.
  • Report progress, tradeoffs, and impact to senior stakeholders on a monthly planning rhythm.
By month 6

The outcome

  • A few automation products in regular use that make the People Team faster and more accurate.
  • Documented, auditable workflows with clear owners, built on integrations that hold up as the company grows.
  • A prioritized backlog and a measured record of what improved for the team and for employees.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with People leaders.
